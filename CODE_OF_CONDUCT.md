# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Criticise the code, not the person.

This is a small project. Most interactions here will be someone reporting that a chat site
changed its layout, or sending a few lines of CSS selectors. That doesn't need much
ceremony — but it does need everyone to be able to participate without being harassed.

## Expected

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, experience levels, and English fluency.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what's best for the project and the people using it.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments — public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Posting other people's conversation content — including in screenshots and DOM fixtures —
  without their consent. This project is about privacy; act like it.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit
comments — and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **admin@aakkagam.com**. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's
identity is not disclosed.

The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete comments, commits, and issues that violate this
document, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they consider
inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Enforcement decisions will be explained where doing
so doesn't compromise someone's privacy.
